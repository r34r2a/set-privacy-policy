Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications published by this developer. It explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we share it, and your rights regarding your data. These apps are offered for educational purposes, personal enrichment, spiritual support, and general entertainment, to serve the needs of users.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit personal identifying information such as:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Government IDs, usernames, or passwords
  • Precise location data (GPS, Wi-Fi, or cell tower)
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone, or sensor data
  • Device serial numbers, IMEI, advertising IDs, or other persistent hardware identifiers

No account creation, login, or registration is required to use the apps.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Certain data may be stored only on your device using local storage. This data is not transmitted to us or any third party:

  • User preferences (theme, text size, language, notification settings)
  • Saved content, bookmarks, notes, or highlights you create
  • Usage history within the app (for example, items you have opened or read)
  • Acceptance status for terms or policies

Because this data remains on your device, we cannot access, view, modify, or delete it. If you clear app data, uninstall the app, or reset your device, this locally stored information will be removed and cannot be recovered.

3. Information Collected Automatically

We do not currently use analytics, tracking pixels, crash reporting, or similar technologies that collect data about how you use the app. If we introduce such technologies in the future, we will update this policy and disclose what is collected, why, and how to opt out where possible.

4. Purchases, Subscriptions, and Payments

The apps are currently offered free of charge. If paid features, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or donations are introduced in the future:

  • Payments will be processed entirely by the app store (such as Google Play or Apple App Store) or a third-party payment provider.
  • We will not collect, store, or process your credit card number, bank account details, or other payment credentials.
  • We may receive only a transaction identifier or confirmation from the app store to verify purchase status and unlock content.
  • Refunds, billing disputes, and subscription management are handled by the app store, not by us.

5. Advertising (Current and Future)

Current status: The apps do not currently display advertisements.

Future possibility: We may introduce advertising in future versions to support development and maintenance. If ads are added, they may be served by third-party advertising networks. Those networks may collect anonymous, non-personally-identifiable information (such as device type, operating system, approximate region, or ad interaction data) in accordance with their own privacy policies. You can limit personalized advertising through your device settings or by opting out where available.

6. How We Use Information

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no information for us to use beyond what is necessary to operate the app locally on your device. Locally stored preferences and content are used only to:

  • Remember your settings and provide a consistent experience
  • Deliver content you have saved or requested
  • Send scheduled notifications if you have enabled them

9. Purpose and Intended Use

The apps are provided for educational purposes, personal enrichment, spiritual support, reading, learning, and general entertainment. They are designed to serve the needs of users seeking accessible content. The apps are not a substitute for professional advice, medical care, legal counsel, or religious authority. Users should verify important information independently.

10. Children's Privacy

The apps are designed for users of all ages, including children, teenagers, and adults. They are provided for educational purposes, personal enrichment, spiritual support, reading, learning, and general entertainment, to serve the needs of users of every age group.

Parental Advisory: We encourage parents and guardians to review the app before allowing a child to use it, and to supervise the child's use of the app. Parents should decide whether the content, features, and any third-party services (such as audio streaming or notifications) are appropriate for their child.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. No account, login, email, name, phone number, or location is required from any user, regardless of age. Any data stored remains only on the device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

Advertising: The apps currently do not display advertisements. However, advertisements may be introduced in future versions. If ads are added, they may be served through third-party advertising networks, which may collect anonymous, non-personally-identifiable information in accordance with their own privacy policies. Parents should be aware of this possibility and may use device settings to limit personalized advertising if desired.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us through the support link in the app or through the app's store listing so we can address it.

11. Data Security

Because we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, there is no central database to breach. Information stored locally on your device is protected only by your device's security measures. We recommend using a device passcode, biometric lock, or encryption to protect your locally stored app data.

12. Your Rights and Choices

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no account-based data for you to access, correct, or delete through us. You may:

  • Clear app data or cache through your device settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored content
  • Disable notifications in the app settings or device settings
  • Limit ad tracking or personalized ads through your device settings

13. Data Retention

Locally stored data is retained only as long as the app remains installed and you do not clear its data. We do not retain copies of your device data on any server.

14. International Users

The apps are made available worldwide. We do not target users in any specific country or region. By using the app, you agree that any locally stored data remains under your control on your device and is not transferred to us.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or app features. The updated version will be posted at the same URL. Your continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

16. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, our data practices, or your rights, you may contact us through the support link in the app or through the contact information provided on the app's store listing.
